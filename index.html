About Me

Who am I?

AI Engineer and Tech Co-Founder specializing in designing, training, and deploying end-to-end intelligent systems. I graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence from Delta University for Science and Technology with Very Good with Honors (CGPA: 3.625). Currently, I am expanding my production-grade AI expertise as a Microsoft Machine Learning Engineer Trainee in the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), focusing on Azure AI workflows, NLP attention models, and MLOps.

My core strength lies in bridging complex algorithmic backends with responsive, user-friendly client interfaces. As Co-Founder and AI Engineer of Lumeria, our graduation project achieved an A+ grade and successfully transitioned into a live production venture launched on the Google Play Store. I built Arabic RAG retrieval systems with LangChain and FAISS, engineered multi-model recommendation engines, deployed low-latency REST APIs using FastAPI, and developed mobile interfaces with Flutter and Supabase.

Beyond technical architecture, I actively advocate for technology and community leadership. In my freshman year, I was awarded the Delta University Shield after delivering the opening keynote for the COP27-aligned AI Climate Innovation Competition among 87 competing teams addressing sustainability challenges. I have served as the Official Stage Presenter for university scientific days and job fairs, while serving as Head of the Voice Over Committee at AI Pioneers and Content Writer for GDG on Campus.
